## Folkestone

To start the holiday we headed across to Folkestone where we stayed a night at the Holiday Inn Express before our early morning booking for Le Shuttle the next morning. After checking into the hotel we noticed that Nova was licking her paw a lot, so decided we should get it checked out.  We managed to find a nice helpful local vet who could see her that evening and it turned out she had a grass seed stuck in between her paw pads.  He managed to remove it and gave her some medicine to stop it from bothering her as much.  We then had Fish and Chips on the sea front and enjoyed exploring the conversion of the station area.

## Utrechtse Heuvelrug National Park

On Monday we headed out to explore the trails in the National Park on our doorstep. We parked by Chalet Helenaheuvel and headed out on the trails from there. The area we walked in was a lovely forest area with clear trails and we walked through the forest to the [Uitkijktoren de Kap](https://www.natuurmonumenten.nl/natuurgebieden/kaapse-bossen/tip/uitkijktoren-de-kaap) and went up to the top, thankfully it was still early so not too hot! At the top there were great views for a long way around 3 sides of the tower - we could see Utrecht and the [Pyramide van Austerlitz](https://www.monumentdepyramidevanausterlitz.nl)

## Sunday 9th

In the afternoon Laura, Kai and Jessica went to Huis Doorn - I dropped them off and then headed back with Nova - we took a scenic route and ended up on some seemingly quite narrow roads with water either side of them!

In the evening Laura and I went out to a local restaurant, [Spott](https://www.spottdoorn.nl) where we had a lovely evening. This was walking distance from where we were staying, had a fantastic atmosphere and we were well looked after by the friendly staff.

Then there was a Portuguese Verdelho served alongside a seafood platter consisting of Oysters, Salmon, Tuna, Dutch Shrimp and Mackerel. We then finished with the Château Cavalier Rosé from Provence along with a hot platter including Nachos, Prawns and Bitterballen. The wines were exquisite and the food was delicious - really good value for money!
