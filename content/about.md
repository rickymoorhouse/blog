I’m a Technical Product Manager for IBM API Connect, where I’m product owner for API Studio. I’ve spent the best part of twenty years at IBM in integration, starting out doing what would now be called SRE before the term really existed, then moving into architecture for our API Connect SaaS offerings, and most recently [into product management](/blog/2026/from-architect-to-product-manager/). That background still shapes how I approach things: I like decisions [grounded in real data](/blog/2026/the-sre-mindset-in-api-architecture/), and I’m always keen to avoid repetitive manual steps. On the technical side, the areas I find most interesting sit at the intersection of how systems are designed, how they interact and how they actually behave under pressure.

This is where I write about that, alongside travel, photography, and walking the dog in the Hampshire countryside. It’s a personal site rather than a focused blog, so expect a bit of everything.

I’m based in the south of England — you can find me on [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/rickymoorhouse) and [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/rickymoorhouse.uk), or use the contact link below.
